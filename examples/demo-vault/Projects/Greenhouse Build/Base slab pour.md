# Base slab pour

Log of the slab.

- Day 1: dig out 250 mm, 3.2 by 4.2 m. Two of us, six hours. Soil to the far corner for the bed later.
- Day 2: hardcore delivered, 2 tonnes, barrowed and whacked in two layers.
- Day 3: shuttering, DPM, mesh on chairs. Rain, so we waited.
- Day 5: pour, 1.4 cubic metres, the mixer lorry could just reach over the fence. Tamped, floated, covered.
- Day 12: shuttering off. One corner slightly low, within 5 mm; the sill plate will take up the rest.

Lesson: order the hardcore for the morning. The afternoon delivery left us whacking in the dark.
