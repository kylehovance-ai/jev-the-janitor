# Greenhouse site decision

**Decision (final):** the greenhouse goes against the south wall of the shed, not in the north corner of the garden.

Why: the north corner is flatter and closer to the tap, but it is shaded by the neighbour's ash from about two in the afternoon from October to March. The south wall gets sun until dusk all year and the wall itself will store heat. Level can be bought with a slab; light cannot.

What this costs: a slab (see [[Base slab pour]]), a longer hose run, and moving the compost bins.

Considered and rejected: a freestanding polytunnel on the lawn (cheap, ugly, blows away), and a window-box cold frame (too small for what I want to grow).
