# Glazing choice

**Decision:** 10 mm twin-wall polycarbonate, not horticultural glass.

- Glass: better light, better looks, heavier, breakable, and the quote was 2,100 for the panes alone.
- Polycarbonate: 640 for the sheets, light enough to fit alone, insulates better, yellows slowly (UV-coated face out slows it).

The look loses to the arithmetic. Revisit in ten years if the sheets have yellowed.
