# Outline

1. Ines inherits the field from an uncle she met twice. The will says nothing about what to do with it.
2. Three offers: the neighbour wants to graze it, the council wants a footpath, a developer wants the corner.
3. She takes the summer to decide, living in the caravan on it.
4. The middle: the field teaches her its history (the old drain, the bomb crater, the orchard that was).
5. She refuses all three and gives the field to itself: a covenant, no building, no ploughing.
6. Coda, ten years on. The path exists anyway, worn by walkers, and she does not mind.
