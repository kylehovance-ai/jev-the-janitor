# Chapter one opening

The field was smaller than the deed said. Ines measured it twice, once with the wheel and once with the app, and both agreed with each other and disagreed with the paper by a little over a fifth of an acre, which was the size, she thought, of a mistake or a gift. She folded the deed back into its envelope and put it in the caravan's one drawer, on top of the tea towels, and went out to look at the hedge.
