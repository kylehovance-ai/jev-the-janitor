# Characters

- **Ines**, forty-one, a surveyor by trade, which is the joke: she measures land for a living and cannot decide what this piece is for.
- **Tomas**, the neighbour. Not a villain. He needs the grazing and says so.
- **The council officer**, never named, always in a hi-vis vest, sincere.
- **Dev**, the developer's agent, who is Ines's cousin, which complicates the dinner in chapter nine.
