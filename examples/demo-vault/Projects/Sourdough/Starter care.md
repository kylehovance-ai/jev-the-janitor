# Starter care

Standing facts about the starter.

- Feed 1:1:1 by weight (starter, flour, water) every 12 hours at room temperature, or once a week from the fridge.
- Room temperature here is 19 to 21°C in winter and 24 to 26°C in summer; the summer starter is ready in eight hours, the winter one in twelve.
- Ready means doubled with a domed top; collapsed and sour means overdue.
- A fridge starter needs two feeds at room temperature before it will raise a loaf.
- Discard goes in the crumpet batter, never the bin.
