# Oven spring troubleshooting

Things that have fixed a flat loaf, in the order I try them:

1. Starter timing (most common). Feed and wait for the dome.
2. Bulk too long. If the dough slumps when tipped out, shorten by 30 minutes next time.
3. Shaping too loose. A tighter boule, seam pinched.
4. Oven not hot. Give it 45 minutes with the pot in.
5. Scoring too shallow. Confident, 1 cm deep, at a low angle.
