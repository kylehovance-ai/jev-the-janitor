# Flour decision

**Decision:** the supermarket strong white at 12.5% protein, not the artisan mill's stoneground.

The stoneground made a better-tasting loaf twice and a brick four times. It absorbs water differently bag to bag and I cannot tell by feel. The supermarket flour is boring and consistent, and consistency is what a weekly loaf needs. The stoneground is for the weekend when I have the attention for it.
