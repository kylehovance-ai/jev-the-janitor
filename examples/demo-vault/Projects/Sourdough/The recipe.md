# The recipe

The weekly loaf, as of batch 14. This is the one that works; change one thing at a time.

- 500 g strong white flour (see [[Flour decision]])
- 375 g water (75%), plus 15 g held back
- 100 g active starter
- 10 g salt

1. Mix flour and 375 g water, rest 45 minutes.
2. Add starter, mix; add salt with the held-back water, mix.
3. Bulk 4 to 5 hours at 24°C with four sets of stretch-and-folds in the first two hours.
4. Shape, cold proof 12 to 16 hours.
5. Bake in the lidded pot at 250°C for 20 minutes, lid off at 230°C for 22 minutes.
