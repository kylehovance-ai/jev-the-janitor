# Running

Standing plan: three runs a week, one long, one easy, one with some effort. The river loop is 5.1 km; the long route over the hill is 11.4 km.

Shoes last about 700 km for me; the current pair started at 0 in [[Running log]].

Resting heart rate has drifted from 62 to 55 over two years of this. That is the only number I track that I trust.
