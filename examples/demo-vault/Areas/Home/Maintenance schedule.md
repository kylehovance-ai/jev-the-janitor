# Maintenance schedule

- Gutters: clear in November and April.
- Boiler service: every September. Engineer's number 555-555-0177.
- Smoke alarms: test monthly, batteries in January.
- Stopcock: behind the panel under the stairs. Turn it once a year so it does not seize.
- Chimney: sweep every other autumn.
