# Measuring Land

Reading notes. A history of surveying; read for the novel, kept for its own sake.

## What I took from it

- Surveying is the history of people disagreeing about where a line is.
- The chain, the wheel and the app all measure a different field.
- Read for the novel; kept because the chapter on hedges as boundaries is beautiful.

## Quotes

> "A boundary is an agreement wearing the costume of a fact."
