# Writing the Middle

Reading notes. On the middle of novels, where mine sagged.

## What I took from it

- The middle sags when the protagonist stops wanting something.
- Give the middle its own small ending; mine now has the crater.
- The checklist at the back is on the wall above the desk.

## Quotes

> "The middle is where the book finds out whether it meant it."
