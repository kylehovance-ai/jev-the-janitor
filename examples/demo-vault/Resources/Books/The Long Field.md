# The Long Field

Reading notes. A novel about land and inheritance; the title I keep borrowing for my own draft, which is a problem I will have to solve.

## What I took from it

- The inheritance is the excuse; the argument over what a field is for is the book.
- Every offer for the land is reasonable, which is what makes refusing them cost something.
- I keep borrowing the title for my own draft, and at some point I will have to stop.

## Quotes

> "Land does not ask what it is for. People do, and then they ask the land to agree."
