# Slow Running

Reading notes. The case for running slower than feels right; it worked.

## What I took from it

- Run slower than feels right and the long route stops being a punishment.
- The heart-rate argument convinced me more than the mileage one.
- It worked: the hill route went from 70 minutes to 58 in a year without any hard sessions.

## Quotes

> "Speed is what happens when you stop trying to buy it on Tuesdays."
