# Flour Water Salt Time

Reading notes. A bread book; the timing tables are the useful part, the philosophy less so.

## What I took from it

- The timing tables are the useful part: bulk at 24°C is two hours shorter than at 20°C, and I had been ignoring that.
- The philosophy chapters can be skipped without loss.
- Weigh the salt. I had been guessing and blaming the flour.

## Quotes

> "Bread is a clock you can eat, and the kitchen sets the clock, not the recipe."
