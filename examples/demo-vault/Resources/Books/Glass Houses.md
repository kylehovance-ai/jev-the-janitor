# Glass Houses

Reading notes. A practical book on small greenhouses: siting, thermal mass, ventilation; the chapter on water barrels changed my plan.

## What I took from it

- Site the house for winter light, not summer; summer looks after itself.
- Thermal mass is a barrel of water, not a clever material; forty litres per square metre of glazing is the rule of thumb.
- The ventilation chapter changed my plan from one roof vent to two plus a low louvre.

## Quotes

> "A greenhouse is a machine for turning morning sun into afternoon warmth, and most of them leak it back by dusk."
